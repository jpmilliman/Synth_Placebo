# Experimental methods tracker

Methods for inference with the LASSO synthetic control that were developed and
tested in this project, in the order they were developed. **Add a new row each
time a method has been tested.**

- **Date developed:** the date the method was first implemented, taken from the
  creation dates of the function and script files.
- **Files not in the repository:** scripts under `Placebo simulations/` and `Example_cases/` (examples of the earlier LASSO and bootstrap functions) are kept locally and are not in the public repository; the simulations behind the current method are in `simulations/`.
- **Simulation / test files:** paths are relative to the project root. Unless
  stated otherwise, simulations use the Kansas data (augsynth) with Kansas
  dropped, each of the other 49 states as the fake treated unit, a fake
  treatment in 2010 Q1, 16 post-treatment quarters, injected step effects, and
  95% intervals.
- **Functions:** `synthetic_control_functions/synthetic_control_functions_claude.R`
  (`meboot_multi()`, `synth_lasso_boot()`, `synth_lasso_placebo()`). Methods
  marked *test only* are not yet in that file.
